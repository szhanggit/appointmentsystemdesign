Chains & stores

Read-only platform view — chains, their stores, plan and quota usage. V1 note: registration is self-service, so there is no store-approval queue — a chain appears here the moment it signs up.

Quota is counted per chain (100 bookings / month on the Free plan), not per store — cancelled and no-show bookings still count. Warnings at 75 / 90 / 100%; at 100% new bookings are hard-blocked until the next cycle. Unused quota does not roll over.
